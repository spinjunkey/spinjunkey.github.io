WHEN TO BRING ME IN

↗

You might be solving
the wrong problem.

Before committing more time, money, or energy, we investigate what’s actually happening, challenge the assumptions underneath the work, and find the problem that’s really worth solving.

You have plenty of information.
You just can’t see the signal.

Research. Interviews. Surveys. Strategy decks. Data. Opinions. More information doesn’t automatically create understanding. I find the patterns, tensions, contradictions, and insights that matter—then translate them into clear implications.

You know what you want to do.
You can’t make it usable.

I turn abstract thinking into tangible forms people can understand, align around, and act on— frameworks, narratives, experiences, prototypes, requirements, working sessions, and experiments.
