INTEL DIGITAL HEALTH

They thought they had an interface problem.

We discovered a product-development problem.

THE SITUATION

A complex digital-health platform was underperforming. The interface was difficult to use, and the natural instinct was to redesign screens.

THE ORIGINAL FRAME

The assumption: the interface was the problem. Build a better interface and the experience would improve.

THE REFRAME

Through research and cross-functional workshops, we uncovered the real issue: human understanding entered product development too late. The deeper problem was the process itself.

WHAT WE DID

We reframed the challenge around upstream discovery, earlier user involvement, shared understanding across disciplines, and a clearer path from insight to requirements and prototypes.

THE RESULT

The team gained a more human-centered way to shape products before expensive decisions became difficult to change.

INTEL EMPLOYEE EXPERIENCE

They thought they needed better employee surveys.

We explored an entirely different way of listening.

THE SITUATION

Employee experience was being measured periodically, which meant issues could sit undetected for weeks or months.

THE ORIGINAL FRAME

The obvious answer was to improve surveys or run them more often.

THE REFRAME

Instead of asking how to measure sentiment more frequently, we asked how the organization might sense experience while it was happening.

WHAT WE DID

We explored real-time signals, moments that mattered, response patterns, and ways to turn listening into a living capability rather than a recurring measurement exercise.

THE RESULT

The organization could identify emerging issues earlier and respond on a much shorter cycle.

FORMFACTOR

They thought they needed employee programs.

We designed the experience of becoming part of the organization.

THE SITUATION

A new manufacturing facility created an opportunity to shape onboarding, communication, recognition, workplace practices and employee experience.

THE ORIGINAL FRAME

The initial question was which programs and processes should be created for the new site.

THE REFRAME

We shifted the question from “What programs should we build?” to “What should it feel like to become part of FormFactor here?”

WHAT WE DID

We connected culture, employee needs, operations, HR, communications and facilities into an ideal-state experience across the employee lifecycle.

THE RESULT

The work created a shared experience vision and translated it into actionable requirements and priorities across functions.
