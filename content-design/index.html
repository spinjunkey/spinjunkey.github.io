typeself-directed content design study
statusin development
friction

A health product can show someone a number and still leave them with four questions: Is this good? Why did it change? Should I worry? What do I do now?

shift

the job isn’t to display the metric. the job is to make the metric mean something.

approach

Start with the question in the user’s head. Explain the signal in plain language. Add context. Separate meaningful change from noise. End with a useful next action without pretending the data knows more than it does.
